Flat warts are small, flat warts caused by human papillomavirus (HPV). Scratching or shaving moves the virus to nearby skin, so they spread in lines. They are easiest to clear when there are only a few. For people whose warts keep coming back, it also helps to look at how run-down the body is, not only to remove what is visible.

Flat warts at a glance

Also calledPlane warts, verruca plana, facial warts
CauseHuman papillomavirus (HPV) infection
AppearanceFlat bumps 1 to 5 mm across, skin-coloured, light brown or light pink
WhereForehead, cheeks, jawline, neck; also chest, belly, back, pelvis
Typical signsPainless; spread in lines along scratches or shaving
When they increaseFatigue, poor sleep, stress, after pregnancy and childbirth
Often confused withSmall acne bumps, freckles, skin tags, milia, seborrheic keratosis, syringoma

Why do flat warts appear and spread?

The virus enters through tiny, invisible cuts and grows in the top layer of skin. Because it is a virus, it spreads, and it comes back when the body is tired. That is the biggest difference from skin tags and seborrheic keratosis.

  • Scratching and touching carries the virus to nearby skin, which is why warts line up along a scratch.
  • Shaving and exfoliating make constant tiny cuts on the jawline, neck and legs. Scrubs and peels do the same.
  • Fatigue and low immunity weaken the skin's ability to keep the virus down.
  • Pregnancy and childbirth often bring a sudden increase.
  • Sharing towels or razors can pass them to family members.

Removal options at our clinic

Diagnosis by the doctor

Flat warts, skin tags, milia, seborrheic keratosis and small acne bumps are told apart one by one before treatment.

Sayul needle removal

Lifts off only the wart tissue with no heat damage. Recovery in about 3 days. Within the chosen area (face or neck), even small ones are removed with no limit on number.

Laser removal

CO2 laser for many warts over a wide area such as the chest, belly or back. Recovery in about 2 weeks.

Herbal medicine for recurrence

For warts that keep returning after several removals, herbal medicine is prescribed to support the immune response.

Skin tags at the same time

Skin tags, milia and seborrheic keratoses nearby can be removed on the same day. See skin tags and milia.

Warts in the genital area, bumps that grow quickly or bleed, and spots whose colour or shape changes irregularly may not be flat warts. These should be diagnosed and, if needed, biopsied at a dermatology or other appropriate clinic first. If you take medicine that lowers immunity, please tell us in advance.

Recovery and recurrence vary with the number, location and your overall condition.

Before and after removal

  • Don't scratch or pick. The virus spreads along anything your nails touch.
  • Leave scabs alone. Pulling them off can leave marks.
  • Pause shaving and exfoliating on the treated area until it heals.
  • Skip saunas, swimming, hard exercise and alcohol for about a week.
  • Use sunscreen carefully to prevent pigmentation.
  • Use your own towel and razor, and get enough sleep to reduce recurrence.
